Privacy Policy

Photographer app · Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Data controller

This app is developed and operated by Halil İbrahim Yürük, who is the data controller for your account information under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Contact: hibrahimyuruk@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password (the password is stored in an unreadable form).
  • Your studio settings: display name, shoot types, default delivery time and currency, reminder message templates.
  • Job records you enter: the name, phone number and email of your client and their partner; shoot date, time, address and meeting point; notes; selected photo codes; job price and payments received.
  • Team information: team members' names, email addresses, roles and their shares from jobs.
  • Your subscription: if you buy a subscription, which plan you bought, when it expires and the number Apple assigns to that subscription. Your card and payment details never reach us.

We do not collect your location, your photos, your full contact list, device identifiers or usage statistics. The app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics tools.

3. Your clients' data

You enter your clients' information into the app yourself. For that data you are the data controller; we store it only on your behalf, in order to provide the service to you. Informing your clients and obtaining their consent where required is your responsibility.

4. Why we process data

  • To create your account and let you sign in
  • To save your jobs, payments and team, and show them across your devices
  • To produce your reports and receivables figures
  • To send verification and password reset codes by email
  • To verify your subscription and apply your plan to your studio

Legal basis: establishing and performing the service relationship between you and us (KVKK Art. 5/2-c) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 5/2-f). We do not use your data for marketing and we do not sell it.

5. Permissions on your phone

  • Calendar: If you allow it, your shoots are written to a separate calendar that the app creates in your phone's calendar. Your other calendars are not read, and no calendar information is sent to our servers. Depending on your phone's settings, this calendar may sync with your own calendar account, such as iCloud.
  • Contacts: With “Choose from Contacts”, only the name, phone number and email of the person you pick are copied into the form. If you choose to add a client to your contacts, the phone's own new contact screen opens.
  • Notifications: Shoot, delivery and payment reminders are scheduled on your phone; no information is sent to our servers for this.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone's Settings.

6. Where data is stored and transfers

Your data is stored on servers in the European Union (Ireland) operated by our infrastructure provider, Supabase. Verification and password reset emails are sent through Google's email service. These providers process data only to deliver the service. Because the servers are located outside Türkiye, your data is transferred abroad.

Subscription payments are taken by Apple through your App Store account. The purchase record Apple creates is passed from the app to our server, and we confirm with Apple that the subscription is valid. The payment itself is subject to Apple's privacy terms.

Apart from this, your data is not shared with anyone unless required by law.

If you place a call or open WhatsApp, email or maps from the app, the relevant phone number, email address or shoot address is passed to that app; from then on that app's own privacy terms apply.

7. Team members

Team members you add to your studio who use the app can see the client and shoot details of the jobs they are assigned to; they cannot see job prices or payments. Admins can see all of the studio's data.

8. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Each studio's data can be accessed only by that studio's users; this rule is enforced at the database level.

9. Retention and account deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings > Delete Account:

  • If you are the studio's only admin, all of the studio's data (jobs, payments, team, settings) is permanently deleted together with your account.
  • If you are a team member, your sign-in account is deleted; your name and email remain in the studio's team list as “removed”. To have these deleted as well, you can contact your studio admin or us.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple; to stop being charged, cancel your subscription on your phone under Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Deleted data may remain in system backups for a short time. Events the app wrote to your phone's calendar stay on your phone; you can delete them from the calendar app.

10. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information, to have it corrected or deleted, to learn whom it has been transferred to, to object to processing, and to claim compensation for damages. You can send your request to hibrahimyuruk@gmail.com; it will be answered within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

11. Children

The app is intended for professional photographers and is not directed at anyone under 18.

12. Changes

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published on this page and the date above will change.
