Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password.
Tap Forgot Password on the sign-in screen. Use the code sent to your email to set a new password.

What is included for free?
You can add 5 new jobs every month; the count resets on the 1st of each month. Jobs you have already added and all your data always stay available. Team features need the Small Team, Studio or Large Studio plan.

How do I subscribe?
In the app, go to Settings > Subscription to see the plans and subscribe. The studio admin subscribes; team members don't pay.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or use Manage Subscription under Settings > Subscription in the app. After you cancel, your subscription lasts until the end of the period and then you return to free use; your data is not deleted. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription.

I switched to a new phone and my subscription doesn't show.
Sign in with the same email address; the subscription belongs to your studio. If it still doesn't show, tap Restore Purchases under Settings > Subscription.

I'd like a refund.
Subscription payments are collected by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I add someone to my team?
Tap Settings > Team > Add Person. If the person will use the app, turn on Will use the app and enter their email; they join your team once they download the app and sign up with that email.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, tap Settings > Delete Account. If you are the studio's only admin, all of the studio's data is permanently deleted as well.
